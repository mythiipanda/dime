# Player Comparison

Use when the user names two or more players with compare, versus, better,
or asks who is greater, more valuable, or worth more in a matchup.

Compare within the same season unless the user asks for a cross-era take.
When comparing across eras, say so explicitly and flag that pace, rules,
and competition level all differ — no number translates cleanly.

Frame every comparison as efficiency versus volume. PPG tells you how
much; TS% tells you at what cost. A 25-PPG scorer at 55% TS is a different
beast than 22 PPG at 62% TS, and the "better scorer" verdict depends on
which question the user is actually asking.

Same-season baselines: compare each player to the league average that
year before comparing them to each other, so you know whether a gap is
big or just two good seasons.

Matchup context matters for head-to-head: who guarded whom, team
strength, and health all shade raw results.

What NOT to do: never compare across eras silently; never reduce a
comparison to one stat; never ignore usage — equal efficiency at higher
usage is strictly better.
